01 · What it isL’Après is the after-party of the market.

Nights of Wonder is RASA’s family holiday market under the Transamerica Pyramid, open 6 to 10 PM. On Fridays and Saturdays, once the market closes at 10, the AMA space turns into L’Après: an adults-only, alpine-styled Hi-Fi Listening Lodge until 2 AM. “Après” as in après-ski, the drinks after the slopes. Here it’s the night after the market.

Each night is an L’Après Session: a guest artist or selector playing records, a local mixologist with a signature cocktail matched to the sound, and stories behind the music. Entry is by RSVP with a cash bar, for about 200 people through the night. In RASA’s deck the room was sketched as “Château Après” with the same lodge line on a backlit sign over the door, which is why the logo has to work in neon or backlit.

03 · Your notesWhat changed from Round 1

Round 1 is kept at the bottom of the page.

My favourite is the Spark accent.

The spark over the E is now the core of every logo, from the wordmark to the flags, seals and icon.

Remove the cursive ones.

The scripts are gone. Everything is set in capitals or a serif lowercase.

Don’t do the tonearm apostrophe.

Removed. Every mark uses the font’s own apostrophe: L’APRÈS, never LAPRÈS.

I liked “Hi-Fi Listening Lodge”.

It’s now the standing line, set between hairlines like the door sign in RASA’s deck.

I like the flags.

Four flags: a felt pennant, a pennant with the lodge line, a swallowtail burgee and a single-line pennant for neon.

The circles need more separation.

Wider gaps between rings, arc text and the centre, and fewer elements in each seal.
